# Case Study 4: Healthcare Electrification in Uganda

## The brief

The Ministry of Health in Uganda has **$1,000,000** for a healthcare electrification programme and must decide where to focus it. Use Energy Access Explorer to combine evidence on healthcare needs and electricity supply, identify priority areas, and justify a funding plan. Submit a report of 4 pages.

## Questions

**The problem.** Why does electrifying health facilities matter for health outcomes in Uganda, and what decision does the Ministry face?

**Method.** Which Energy Access Explorer layers, filters, weights and prioritisation index did you use? Justify each choice for both demand and supply.

**Priority areas.** Which districts rank highest? Present your ranking and explain what drives the top five.

**Budget allocation.** How would you spend the $1,000,000? State your cost per facility (with a source), any maintenance reserve, and the facilities and people reached.

**Sensitivity and trade-offs.** How does your plan change if costs are higher or lower? Where does need conflict with value for money (cost per person)?

**Limitations and recommendations.** What data gaps or assumptions could change your answer, and what should the Ministry do next?
